    MESSAGE

    01 — FROM OUR TEAM

    Good cars deserve a second life — and the right driver, wherever they are.

    Japan has one of the most demanding vehicle inspection regimes in the world. Cars are serviced on schedule, garaged, and often replaced long before they are worn out. Many of them are right-hand drive models that were never sold new in Britain.

    Japan car cycle exists to connect those vehicles with drivers in the UK. We listen first — your request sheet tells us exactly what you want and what you won’t accept — then we search, inspect and ship with the same care you would take yourself.

    Cross-border buying should feel simple and transparent. One point of contact, honest auction reports, clear costs, and a car that arrives as described. That is the cycle we promise to keep.
